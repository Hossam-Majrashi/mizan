# Privacy Policy / سياسة الخصوصية

*Last updated: October 17, 2026*

## English

### 1. Introduction
Mizan ("we", "our", or "the app") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains our practices regarding user information.

### 2. Zero Data Collection
Mizan does **not** collect, store, transmit, or share any personal data, identifiers, usage statistics, or telemetry.

### 3. Local-Only Processing
All video analysis, metadata extraction, and frame comparison calculations occur 100% locally on your device. Selected video files never leave your device and are never uploaded to any remote server or third-party service.

### 4. No Network Connections
The application contains no internet permissions in its primary configuration and makes no network requests.
